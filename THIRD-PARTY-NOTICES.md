# Third-party materials

Endfield Widget is an unofficial project for Arknights: Endfield.

Game names, characters, portraits in `assets` and original announcement text in the test fixtures belong to their respective rights holders. They are not original artwork or writing created by this project.

The project's [MIT License](LICENSE) covers original project code, interface translations and documentation. It does not cover these third-party materials or original official content retrieved at runtime, and does not grant permission to reuse them.

Portraits and saved public fixtures come from official sources:

- [Arknights: Endfield](https://endfield.gryphline.com/en-us)
- [Official announcements](https://endfield.gryphline.com/en-us/news), including their available localized editions
- [Official operator catalog](https://endfield.gryphline.com/en-us/operator)
- Official content domains: `web-static.hg-cdn.com` and `web-news.gryphline.com`.

Fixtures contain public articles and an operator catalog page, not private SKPORT account responses. Personal credentials are configured locally in the application.

The Simplified Chinese article display uses the Windows `LCMapStringEx` script conversion API on the global Traditional Chinese edition. Original article links retain their official edition.
